 Hello Kitty was created in Japan in 1974 by illustrator Yuko Shimizu for the Japanese lifestyle brand Sanrio.

Creation and Early History

The Concept: Shintaro Tsuji, the founder of Sanrio, wanted to add a new character to his line of cute merchandise to boost social communication.

First Product: The character—named Kitty White—made her retail debut in 1975 on a small, clear vinyl coin purse.

The Design: Shimizu designed her as a white cat with a signature red bow and no mouth. The lack of a mouth was an intentional choice so people could project their own feelings onto her.

Backstory and Identity

Not Just a Cat: While she is styled after a Japanese Bobtail cat, Sanrio's official character biography describes her as a cheerful little British girl.

London Roots: Her backstory places her birth in the suburbs of London, England, where she lives with her parents and twin sister, Mimmy.

The Name: Her name was inspired by the cat named "Kitty" in Lewis Carroll's Through the Looking Glass, combined with "Hello" to emphasize the brand's focus on communication.

Fun Facts

Blood Type: Sanrio assigns her the rare fictional blood type A.

Punishment in Bangkok: In 2007, police officers in Bangkok, Thailand who committed minor infractions were briefly ordered to wear Hello Kitty armbands as a shaming punishment.

Themed Maternity Hospital: There is a fully Hello Kitty-themed maternity hospital in Taiwan where rooms, nurse uniforms, and decor feature the character.
