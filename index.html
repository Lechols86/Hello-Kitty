Hello Kitty

Overview & Identity

• Creation: Debuted in November 1974, first appearing on a vinyl coin purse in 1975.

• Species vs. Human: While widely depicted with feline features like whiskers and cat ears, Sanrio has clarified in official lore that Hello Kitty is actually a bright, kind-hearted little girl rather than a literal cat.

• Physical Traits: Said to stand five apples tall and weigh three apples. She wears a red bow on her left ear and is famously depicted without a visible mouth.
